Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2025

Support Terms of Use

NightLight (“we”, “our”, “us”) does not collect or store any personal data on its own servers. Some limited data (like your current location) is processed locally on your device or transmitted securely to third-party map providers solely to provide app functionality. This policy explains what limited information the app processes and how it is used.

Personal data we collect

  • Location data: We use your device location to show nearby routes and lamps, calculate directions and share a link via SMS if you choose. Location is processed while you use those features.
  • Diagnostics: Our servers (Cloudflare Workers) may temporarily process your IP address and basic request metadata to operate the service and prevent abuse. We do not build profiles.
  • Purchases: Apple processes your subscription. We receive purchase validation information (receipt verification results) necessary to confirm an active subscription. We do not see your payment card details.

How we use your data

  • Provide core features (maps, routing, lamp overlays, reporting).
  • Validate subscriptions and enable paid features.
  • Keep the service secure and reliable (rate limiting, debugging).

Data sharing

  • Apple: In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We verify subscription receipts with Apple’s servers.
  • Map providers: Routing/map requests may be sent to third-party APIs (e.g., Mapbox, Overpass). These providers receive network metadata (e.g., IP) as part of normal web requests.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention

We retain only what’s necessary to operate the service. Routing/lamps requests are not stored long-term. Subscription validation tokens are short-lived.

Your controls

  • You can revoke location permission in your device settings at any time (note: features may not work without it).
  • To delete support emails, contact support@nightlightapp.com.

Children

NightLight is not directed to children under 13. Parents or guardians may choose to help minors use it under supervision.
